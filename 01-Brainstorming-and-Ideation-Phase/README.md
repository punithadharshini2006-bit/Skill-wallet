# Brainstorming & Ideation Phase

## Project Name
Skill-wallet

## Introduction
Skill-wallet is a project designed to help users manage and develop their skills in a structured way.

## Problem Identified
Students and learners often find it difficult to organize their skills, track their learning progress and identify areas where they need improvement.

## Proposed Idea
The Skill-wallet project aims to provide a platform where users can manage their skills, learning activities and related information in an organized manner.

## Objectives
- To provide a platform for managing skills.
- To help users track their learning progress.
- To organize skill-related information.
- To provide a simple and user-friendly system.

## Target Users
- Students
- Learners
- Skill developers
- Job seekers

## Expected Outcome
A simple and useful skill management platform that helps users organize and monitor their skills.
